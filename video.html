I have here collected videos that I have made from 2011 and onwards some where in group shows and screening and some where never exhibited and has later been finished.

SECRETS 2018

With this work I was thinking about the language of online advertisment, trolls and chatbots that we now call AI and other skammers using the language of care and mindfullness (that also can be part of a manipulating industry). By mentioning how someone may feel, human psycology is easily fooled to belive it is their own feelings and quickly a feeling of anexity can be created, that can be used in purpose of making users dependent for continued extraction and manipulation. I added retro computer music in the end of the video as I was thinking of this idea being such an "old trick" at the same time retro aestetics is another way of masking something as familular. The footage is looped images from Shutterstock as I was intrested in using generic images and stock footage.

Keyworks3 2022
