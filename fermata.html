Fermata, (2021) a collaboration between Vera Karlsson and Iman Mohammed. The video is a result from our meeting at Gotland film institute for four days during summer 2021. The work documents Mohammed's memories and relationship to the iland and specifically the peninsula Fårö. Gotland is also the place where Karlsson grew up and our friendship was formed later in Stockholm. The work discuss the implications of belonging to a place and what that means and is a response to the political climate of Sweden and Europe. It is also a portrait of Mohammed as a poet, and includes cinema and music that inspires her work, David Lynch's Lost Highway is quoted in the film and it includes footage from other cinematic works; Disney’s Alice in Wonderland and Cuadecuc vampir by Pier Portabellos and a song from Les Rallizes Dénudés - Strung out deeper than the night. The title comes from Mohammed's second poetry collection, Fermata (2018) which she reads from.

Camera, sound and edit, Vera Karlsson.
Thank you Film på Gotland for support.
