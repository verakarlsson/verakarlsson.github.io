Working with a caravan as studio, installation and body, field recordings, video, fotoalbums and more to create the art of 'Nancy Halt'. Whos life builds upon another artists, the exceptional Land Work artist, Nancy Holt.

The work drew upon mythology and art-historical references to create a fictional character and artist. The project appropriates Land Art as it's study and their life’s work—from abtuction and birth and through tropes such as family, adolescence, the concept of leisure, alien life and work.
