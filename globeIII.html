Globe II, (2026) showed in the group exhibition, see Porten at Gotlands Konstmuseum and Skulptur Projekte 3.0 at Beau Travail, Stockholm.

The video "Globe II" (2026) is a reworking of a video piece I created in 2018. The footage was captured using an iPhone at an airport (Arlanda) and focuses on automatic doors and escalators. The text in the video describes the action in a directive manner. It is loosely based on the philosopher Henri Bergson’s (1859–1941) account of the intellect’s function - how it comprehends and navigates the physical world by transforming the continuous flow of reality into discrete images, and how our brains convert "sensory impressions" into "motor reactions" (actions). This, in turn, parallels how AI and machine learning utilize images to understand the world.
I am interested in how Bergson’s concepts are interpreted and experienced within a contemporary context, in relation to the filmed environment, the technology employed, and the ways in which navigational information is conveyed and received (affect).
The video is also influenced by Gilles Deleuze’s film theory, “Cinema 1” where Bergsons philosophy is deployed through cinema. As well as by the way images are constructed through the optics of a mobile camera, a process that, to some extent, algorithmically anticipates imagery (based on preceding frames) to achieve sharp visual quality, thereby rendering the system, in a sense, blinded.
