Collages in Keywork two of five scans, collages in Keywork Unless useless, video with sound Keywork at bbberlin , 2022

The video, 'Unless useless' follows a fractured everyday narrativ taking place in a made-up universe where all visible human labour have been replaced by automation. Each still in the video can be distributed and sold as a unique inkjet poster with added doodles by Vera. Together the posters creates a puzzle and a shared whole between shareholders.

The video unless useless is a fictional work taking inspiration from french Nouvelle Vague cinema. It was dealing with the covid-19 time of isolation and a reality where most visible human labour has been replaced by machines.
