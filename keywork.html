Unless useless
video with sound, 2022

Keywork at bbberlin

The video, 'Unless useless' follows a fractured everyday narrativ taking place in a made-up universe where all visible human labour have been replaced by automation. Each still in the video can be distributed and sold as a unique inkjet poster with added doodles by Vera. Together the posters creates a puzzle and a shared whole between shareholders. (pressrelese 2022)

The video *Unless Useless* is a fictional work that draws inspiration from French New Wave cinema, reflecting both the present day and a bygone era. The footage was captured in a privately owned apartment that formerly was part of a social housing development. It explores the isolation experienced during the COVID-19 pandemic, and a reality most visible human labor has been replaced by automation.

It's theamatic was continued in the exhibition Keyworks.
