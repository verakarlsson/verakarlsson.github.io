SECRETS, 2018

I was thinking about the language of online advertisment and chatbots, adopting the language of care and mindfullness. I am interested in how human psycology is mirroring itself in others and how that is used for extraction and manipulation. I added retro computer music in the end of the video as I was thinking of this idea being an "old trick" at the same time retro aestetics is another way of masking something as familular. The footage is looped images from Shutterstock as I was intrested in using generic images and stock footage.
